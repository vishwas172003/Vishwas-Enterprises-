How a project runs

The same five steps for a single service bay or a full factory air network.

  1. Site survey

    A free visit to walk the workshop, note the bay layout, the tools in use and where the compressor and oil storage will sit.

  2. Layout and quotation

    A piping and equipment layout suited to your site, with a quotation based on it.

  3. Supply

    Pipe, fittings, compressors, FRL units, reels and equipment from the brands we work with.

  4. Installation

    Our team installs, aligns and connects the complete system with secure supports and neat routing.

  5. Testing and handover

    Leak testing and commissioning before handover, then AMC and technical support when you need it.

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Call or message us and we'll answer directly.

Is the first site visit free?

Yes. We offer a free initial site visit to understand your workshop layout and requirement before sharing a quotation.

Do you work outside Pune?

Yes. Besides Pune, we regularly work in Chakan, Ranjangaon, Mumbai, Nashik, Kolhapur, Sangli and Goa, and take on larger projects elsewhere in India.

How long does a typical installation take?

It depends on scope. A standard air piping or FRL installation usually takes 2 to 5 working days once the layout is finalised.

Why choose aluminium air piping over GI pipe?

Aluminium pipe does not rust inside, so the air stays cleaner and filters and tools last longer. It is lighter, has a smooth bore that reduces pressure loss, and its modular fittings make it quicker to install and easier to extend when you add bays. GI pipe can still suit some applications, and we will advise based on your site.

Do you offer AMC (annual maintenance) plans?

Yes. We provide AMC for pneumatic piping, garage tools, compressors and oil management systems, with scheduled preventive maintenance visits.

Can you supply a brand that is not listed here?

In most cases, yes. Send us the brand and model you need and we will confirm availability and installation support.

What should I share to get a quotation?

Your location, the number of service bays or machines, the tools or equipment you run, and photos or a sketch of the layout if you have one. A site visit fills in the rest.
