FAQ

Common questions

Do you work outside Pune?

Yes. We regularly support projects across Mumbai, Nashik, Chakan, Ranjangaon, Kolhapur, Sangli and Goa, in addition to Pune.

Is the site visit free?

Yes, we offer a free initial site visit to understand your workshop layout and requirement before sharing a quotation.

Do you provide AMC (annual maintenance) plans?

Yes, we offer AMC support for pneumatic piping, garage tools, compressors and oil management systems, with scheduled preventive maintenance visits.

How long does a typical installation take?

Timelines depend on scope — a standard air piping or FRL installation typically takes 2–5 working days after the planning stage is finalized.

Can you supply and install equipment from a brand not listed on your site?

In most cases, yes — reach out with the brand and model you need and we'll confirm availability and installation support.
